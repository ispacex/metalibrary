Data

  • comparison.csv: by $n$, $E_2$, the rapacious rule (“low”/“high”), Toda 2003, Toda 1962, family, lecture (the last two columns are in Russian).
  • e2_coloured.csv: $E^2$ by $(n,s,\text{colour})$, $n\le150$ (colour $=Q$-weight).
  • sseq_qweight_S3_p3.csv: the unstable Ext for $S^3$ at $p=3$ by $(n,s,e)$, as printed by qgrade_dims; rows n,s,e,dim, $n=t-s$.
  • e2_metalib.csv: direct enumeration of the metalibrary by blocks $(n,s,\text{colour})$: the number of words, the rank of the differential, the dimension of $E^2$, $n\le64$.
  • sseq_S3_p3.csv: the same Ext from sseq/ext without the weight; rows n,s,dim, $n\le150$.
  • differentials.json: the differentials of the rapacious rule (both fillings and the variant with $d_5$).
  • toda2003_S3.csv: our reading of the $S^3$ column of Toda's 2003 table (the source column is in Russian).
A recomputation for R. Mikhailov's lecture “Coloured metalibraries. The mind of space” (2 September 2016). Data as of 15 October 2026. All numbers on these pages are 3-primary: the rank of the 3-torsion, all summands $\mathbb Z/3$.
