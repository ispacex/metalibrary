E² classes by length and colour

Each class is written as (s, c): $s$ is the length of the word (the Adams filtration), $c$ is the colour (the number of letters $\mu$). The colours up to $n=150$ are the $Q$-weight of Ext, checked cell by cell against a direct computation of the metalibrary (method). “Left” means after the rapacious rule (colour $+1$, “low”). The column “family” gives the class standing in the place of a Toda element ($n=4k+2$) or of a sandwich ($n=4m+1$). The colours of these classes run 3 4 4 5 5 6 …, as stated in the lecture.

The picture goes up to $n=100$; the table below, up to $n=150$.
