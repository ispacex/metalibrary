Comparison by n: the rule, Toda, the lecture

Each row is the group $\pi_n(S^2)=\pi_n(S^3)$ ($n\ge4$), its 3-primary part. A number is the rank of the 3-torsion.

  • E²: the unstable $E_2$ for $S^3$ at $p=3$ without the tower over $\pi_3$, that is, the homology of the metalibrary; the colours are the $Q$-weight of Ext (method), $n\le150$.
  • rapacious: what remains after the rapacious rule with colour $+1$. The two columns are two ways of filling the chains, “low” and “high” (differentials). The result is final up to $n=149$.
  • Toda 2003: our reading of Toda's 2003 table ($n\le82$); Toda 1962: his book ($n\le22$).
The rapacious rule (“low”) agrees with Toda for all $n\le48$. These are exactly the stems $k=n-3<46$ that Toda determined in 1967. Disagreements for $n\le82$: $\pi_{49}$, $\pi_{50}$, $\pi_{51}$, $\pi_{59}$, $\pi_{60}$, $\pi_{63}$, $\pi_{64}$, $\pi_{66}$, $\pi_{67}$, $\pi_{68}$, $\pi_{69}$, $\pi_{70}$, $\pi_{73}$, $\pi_{74}$, $\pi_{75}$, $\pi_{76}$, $\pi_{77}$, $\pi_{79}$, $\pi_{80}$; in every case the rule leaves more classes than Toda. For $49\le n\le149$ the rule gives no zeros in either variant.
