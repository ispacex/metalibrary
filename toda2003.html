Toda's 2003 table

H. Toda, Unstable 3-primary Homotopy Groups of Spheres, Econoinformatics (Himeji Dokkyo University) 29 (2003), 1–77 (NDL BibID 6899969, CiNii crid 1523669554954337536). An OCR'd scan is on D. Ravenel's page: Toda03-ocr.pdf (PDF pages 67–68: §5.6, $k\le55$; 80–81: §6.5, $56\le k\le79$).

On this page: the $S^3$ column (with the alpha families), all columns of both tables, checks, discrepancies; the text of §5 is on its own page.

The $S^3$ column

How we read it. We took only the column $n=3$, that is, $_3\pi_{3+k}(S^3)={}_3\pi_{3+k}(S^2)$. Each dot is a $\mathbb Z/3$ (by the Cohen–Moore–Neisendorfer theorem the exponent of the 3-torsion of $\pi_*(S^3)$ is 3). A row of the table that starts to the right of $n=3$ is not counted.

“mod A”. The table of §6.5 is given modulo the unstable α-families. By §4.3 such a family contributes to $S^3$ one $\mathbb Z/3$ in each of the stems $rq-2$ and $rq-1$ ($q=4$), that is, for $k\equiv2,3\pmod4$: these are the sandwiches and the Toda elements. We added them back (column “+α”). This is the point of our reading to check first.

Checks of the reading: for $n\le22$ it agrees with Toda's 1962 book; for no $n$ does it exceed $E_2$.

All columns

How we read. Every row of both tables was copied from the page images (400–600 dpi); OCR is not used: it confuses $\bullet$ with $\circ$ and loses columns. The reading is kept in the repository as text, a line per printed row (metalibrary/toda03, the format is in toda2003.py), and the groups in the cells are derived from it by the program. The table of §5.6 gives whole groups (alpha families included), the table of §6.5 gives them modulo the unstable alpha families (mod A); in the $S^3$ column above we added $A$ back, in the other columns of §6.5 we did not: that has not been done for them, and the grid of §6.5 below is mod A, as in Toda.

The whole text of §5 (with the diagrams and the table of §5.6 in Toda's notation) is on a page of its own: §5 of the paper.

Toda's notation (§5.2): $\bullet\cong\mathbf Z/3$, $\circ\cong\mathbf Z/9$, $\triangleright\cong\mathbf Z/27$; between neighbouring spheres the double suspension $E^2$: “$=$” is a bijection, “$\to$” is non-trivial and not a bijection ($\bullet\to\circ$, $\circ\to\triangleright$ injective, $\circ\to\bullet$, $\triangleright\to\circ$ surjective, $\circ\to\circ$, $\triangleright\to\triangleright$ multiplication by $p$); “$=\langle x\rangle$”: the same group from there on up to the stable one, $x$ the stable generator. A row that ends without “$=\langle\cdot\rangle$” goes to zero on the next sphere. Summands written under each other are separate summands.

Discrepancies

  1. $\pi_{54}(S^9)$, $k=45$, $n=9$: open. Theorem 5.3 (1) (p. 53, PDF 60) has a removable simple unstable element at $(n,k)=(9,45)$ ($AB_2$ above the dot, $B_2$ below it), so mod A $\pi^9_{54}\supseteq\mathbf Z/9\oplus\mathbf Z/3$; the table of §5.6 (p. 61) at $k=45$, $n=9$ shows only the $\circ$ of $\varphi$, that is $\mathbf Z/9$. The stem $45\equiv1\pmod4$ has no alpha families, so both speak of the whole group. For the theorem: Toda's pairs $ab_2/AB_2$ stand at $(3,32)/(3,33)$, $(9,44)/(9,45)$, $(15,56)/(15,57)$, $(21,68)/(21,69)$, and the tables have all of them but $(9,45)$; every other pair $ab/AB$ of simple elements is in the table of §5.6. Most likely the table of §5.6 misses a dot, and ${}_3\pi_{54}(S^9)=\mathbf Z/9\oplus\mathbf Z/3$. $E_2$ does not decide it: $\dim E_2(S^9)$ in the stem 45 is 8, and the balance of the differentials works out either way. In the grid above the cell is left as in the table and marked red. It is not our misreading: the reviewer re-read the place independently.
  2. Misprints in the text of §5 (no effect on the groups; marked [sic] in the text): $AAB_2$ for $ABB_2$ in (5.1.7); $ab_2^2$ twice in (5.1.8); “(13) of (5.5.1)” for (5.1.1) on p. 46; $\beta^4$ for $\beta_1^4$ and $y=b_2^2$ for $b^2b_2$ in (5.1.11); “und” in Proposition 5.3; $\pi^5_{7+k}$ for $\pi^7_{7+k}$ on p. 49; $H_p(\partial\alpha(\xi))$ in Lemma 5.2; the label $E^2$ for $E_2$ in the diagram of Theorem 5.4 (2), $k=53$; in the same diagram at $k=55$ the print has $\bullet=\bullet\hookleftarrow\bullet$, whereas the table of §5.6 and the rows $k=43,45$ of Theorem 5.3 give $\bullet\hookleftarrow\bullet=\bullet$; in the table of §5.6 the stable name at $k=49$ is $\alpha_1\beta_1\beta_2$ for $\alpha_1\beta_1^2\beta_2$ ((17) of (5.1.1)).
  3. The scan: the page “(3) Let $\xi=\alpha_1(7)\circ\varphi(10)$ …” is printed twice (PDF 65 and 66, numbered 58 and 59, two printings of one text); in the table of §6.5 the chains of $k=74$ ($\beta_5$) and $k=75$ ($\mu$) run one symbol into the header “stable”, which changes no group.
  4. Names, not numbers. Toda's stable generators and those of Ravenel's Table A3.2 have different names: $\epsilon'=\langle\alpha_1,\alpha_1,\beta_1^3\rangle$, $\epsilon_1=\beta_{3/2}$, $\epsilon_2=\beta_3$, $\varphi=x_{45}$, $\lambda=x_{68}$, $\mu=x_{75}$; the orders agree everywhere (check B). There is no disagreement with Toda 1962 or with the stable stems.
